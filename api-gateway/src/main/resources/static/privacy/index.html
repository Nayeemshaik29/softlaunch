3. Sensitive information

Some things you share can reveal very personal details: your gender and who you're interested in can reveal your sexual orientation, and your photos, date of birth, location and ID verification are personal too. We use them only to run SoftLaunch as described here. We never sell them or use them for advertising. Your sexual orientation is never shown as a label; other members only see you if you match their preferences and they match yours.

4. AI and automatic checks

  • Scam and fraud checks: new chat messages are checked automatically, by rules and by an AI model that runs on our own servers. Your messages are not sent to any outside AI company. If a message looks like a scam (for example asking for money), our safety team gets a report, which they review. The check keeps the message text for at most 24 hours; its result does not contain your text.
  • Chat openers: when you match, our AI can suggest first messages based on both profiles' interests and bio. Suggestions are kept for 30 minutes.
  • "Similar vibe" ranking: we turn your interests, bio, prompts and favourite places into numbers, so people with similar interests can appear higher in your feed. Your name, age, city and what you're looking for are not used for this.
  • These checks don't make final decisions about your account on their own. Actions such as banning an account are taken by people.

5. Who can see your data

  • Other members see your profile: name, age, gender, city, photos, bio and the other profile fields you fill in, a rounded distance, and your "Verified" badge. They don't see who you're interested in, your exact location or your email. Matches can also see your messages and online status.
  • Our safety team sees reports, including the chat messages copied into them.
  • A small number of our staff (admins) can see an account overview: your name, photos, age, city, a partly hidden email, when you were last active and the activity counts above. Admins can't see your messages, calls, exact location, date of birth or ID documents. Every time an admin looks at an account, it's written to an audit log.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and who we've shared it with.
  • Correct or update it. You can edit most of it yourself in the app.
  • Erase it, by deleting your account (section 8).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.
  • Complain to us (section 13), and if you're not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Without the app: delete your account on the web. You'll be signed out everywhere at once, and your data is removed from all our systems within minutes, except what section 6 says we keep (reports and backups).

9. How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Photos are in private storage, shown only through links that expire.
  • Our databases are not reachable from the internet, and access is limited to the people who run the service.
  • Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for a short time.
  • If a data breach could affect you, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

No system is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.
